XC-Flight

Start detection and scoring flight for cross-country flights according to the Free Glide League rules (version 1.0.6). Rewrite based on the app "Free Glide League" by LeonAir RC.

Sequence

  • Ready: turn on the start switch while the model is below the maximum altitude (400 m).
  • Entry time (10 s): at least 5 m above ground, below 400 m, ENL below the limit (300 = 30 %) and continuous sinking. A violation triggers an alarm and the entry time restarts.
  • Scoring flight: GPS distance, average speed and flight time. Time classes at 30 min, 1 h, 2 h, 3 h.
  • Reset switch: ends the measurement, back to "Ready".

Rules during the scoring flight

  • above 700 m (warning altitude): altitude alarm every 5 s
  • above 750 m: INVALID, values frozen
  • motor on before 30 min (ENL above limit and climb over 1 m/s for more than 8 s): INVALID
  • motor on after 30 min: scored up to the last time class reached
  • distance below 5 km: grey with "< 5 km"; three beeps at 5 km

Settings

  • Sensors: ENL, altitude (relative to launch site), GPS latitude and longitude
  • Switches: start, reset
  • Audio and alarms: announce average speed every 10 s (0 = off), audio files for ENL, climb, altitude alarm and start (without file: fallback beeps), start countdown (off/voice/beep)
  • Start rules: entry time 10 s, minimum altitude 5 m, maximum altitude 400 m, ENL limit 300, minimum sink 0.0 m, climb tolerance 0.3 m
  • Scoring flight: warning altitude 700 m, max. scoring altitude 750 m
  • Other: announce km from 5 km

The menu is locked during entry time and flight.

Display

Full-page telemetry window "XC-Flight": during the flight distance, average speed and flight time in large font, during the entry time the countdown, on the right an ENL bar marking the limit.

ENGO 2 glasses

With the app "VSpeak glasses 2" the values appear on the glasses:

  • XC-Flight 1: distance, average speed, flight time
  • XC-Flight 2: altitude, entry timer, ENL

Provided as an aid without warranty. Scoring is done from the IGC file according to the rules.
