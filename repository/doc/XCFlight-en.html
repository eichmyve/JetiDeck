XC-Flight

Start detection and scoring flight for cross-country flights according to the Free Glide League rules (version 1.0.6). Rewrite based on the app "Free Glide League" by LeonAir RC.

Sequence

  1. Ready: turn on the start switch while the model is below the maximum altitude (400 m).
  2. Entry time (10 s): at least 5 m above ground, below 400 m, ENL below the limit (300 = 30 %) and continuous sinking. A violation triggers an alarm and the entry time restarts.
  3. Scoring flight: GPS distance, average speed and flight time. Time classes at 30 min, 1 h, 2 h, 3 h.
  4. Reset switch: ends the measurement, back to "Ready".

The menu is locked during entry time and flight.

Display

Full-page telemetry window "XC-Flight": during the flight distance, average speed and flight time in large font, during the entry time the countdown, on the right an ENL bar marking the limit.

ENGO 2 glasses

With the app "VSpeak glasses 2" (VSGL2) the values appear on the glasses: page "XC-Flight 1" = distance, average speed, flight time; page "XC-Flight 2" = altitude, entry timer, ENL.

Provided as an aid without warranty. Scoring is done from the IGC file according to the rules. Tested in the JETI Studio emulator; a test on the transmitter is still pending.
